§1

The spotlight thesis

Spotlight papers win on one sharp claim, isolated by a causal ablation and cashed out in a downstream payoff. State it as one sentence and make every table serve it.

One-sentence claim

Verifying black-box agent traces as evidence-local obligations with calibrated abstention cuts the false-valid rate 4–16×, the gap widens with horizon, the effect is causally the abstention decision rule (not the checkers), it transfers across two unrelated reward-hack surfaces via only a data adapter — and it moves a real downstream metric.

The first three clauses are already proven (SA-1, the horizon slice, SA-3). The last clause — and the rigor behind the transfer claim — are what currently block spotlight.

The 2026 field you are landing in

This is a crowded subfield. Reviewers will pattern-match AVG to these, so positioning has to be explicit — and the differentiators (locality, abstention, cross-surface transfer) are exactly where these are weakest.

Model-agnostic runtime governance; predicates over traces, veto on violation. arXiv:2606.04104

C-Trace (Runtime Compliance)

Formal predicates over execution traces, runtime rejection. arXiv:2606.19242

Agent-as-a-Judge / AJ-Bench

Evidence-gathering judges — the baseline reviewers will demand you beat. arXiv:2604.18240

Graph of Verification (GoV)

DAG-decomposed verification of reasoning — your "graph" framing. arXiv:2506.12509

The wedge: most of these commit a verdict. Few abstain, few show one mechanism transferring across unrelated failure surfaces. That is AVG's defensible ground — organize the tables around it.

§6

New experiments, prioritized

Ordered by leverage per unit effort. The P0 block is the difference between a solid paper and a spotlight one.

P0

Downstream utility — best-of-N reranking

Select from N sampled trajectories per task with AVG vs. monolith vs. PRM; report task success of the pick. Stronger variant: filter a trace pool and report the filtered-in set's true-success rate.

Why first: highest-leverage single experiment — corpora and verifier arms already exist, and it turns "false-valid" into "cleaner data / better selection."
P0

Two real competitive baselines

An Agent-as-a-Judge (evidence-gathering) arm and a PRM / step-scored arm on the SA-1 setup. Without these, differentiation from the 2026 field is asserted, not shown.

P0

Scale the LLM-judge slice

From n=120 single-seed to n≥500 × 3 seeds (at least on τ-bench) so the ~15× headline survives a rigor challenge.

P1

Third domain for transfer

SWE-Gym via the existing terminal adapter is the low-cost path. A 3×3 diagonal is far more convincing than 2×2 for "adapter necessary, misspec abstains."

P1

Selective-verification frontier

Mostly a re-plot of SA-3 data. High value, low cost — this is the answer to the coverage objection.

P1

Human-review efficiency

Time-to-correct-verdict (or accuracy) with the witness vs. the raw trace on ~50 traces. Lands the "witness compresses the trace" claim the intro promises.

P2

Cost curves + robustness sweep

Cost-normalized performance curves (already promised in Sec 4.7) and a small noisy-log / tool-schema-shift grid for the appendix.

§7

Would more benchmarks & baselines help?

Partly — and unevenly. Baselines are worth adding; benchmarks only barely. Both feel productive, but neither is what converts this to a spotlight. Add a little breadth as insurance; spend the real effort on depth.

LeverAdd?How farWhy
Competitive baselines YES 2–3 specific: PRM, Agent-as-a-Judge, +1 named 2026 verifier Credibility gate vs. the field. Reviewers check the category is represented, not the count — a 4th/5th judge is diminishing returns.
More benchmark domains SPARINGLY +1 (SWE-Gym via existing adapter), then stop A 3×3 transfer diagonal is worth it. Beyond that, each new domain needs an adapter + spec + traces + the reward-hack framing to hold — and is a chance for the clean story to break. Downside-heavy.
Downstream payoff FIRST The load-bearing addition (SA-7) Orthogonal to breadth. This is the item that actually moves the tier.
The decisive point

More benchmarks + more baselines still leaves you as “a better verifier, benchmarked more carefully.” That is a strong poster, not a spotlight. The tier comes from depth on the claim — the causal abstention ablation (you have it) plus a downstream payoff (you don't yet).

One trap to avoid: a poorly-chosen extra domain can actively hurt — it shrinks the headline gap where endpoint checks already suffice, or wrecks the coverage story where there's little mechanical evidence. Your own proposal says gains are largest only where correctness is weakly supervised, so breadth is not free.

§8

Rigor upgrades & honest limits

Rigor reviewers will demand

  • ≥3 seeds, mean±SE, significance test on the FV gap. Terminal-Bench is single-seed today — fix.
  • Reframe calibration: AUROC 0.46/0.60 is a liability if sold as calibration. The label is coarse; the abstain decision is what's calibrated — Fig 2 is the right evidence. Say this before a reviewer weaponizes it.
  • Matched-budget disclosure: put AVG, judges, and PRM on equal token/cost budgets and state it — else "you just spent more compute."

Limits to pre-empt in the paper

  • Offline-replay only — no live agent; caveat the "online intervention" framing.
  • Low absolute coverage by design — own it via the frontier.
  • Trajectory-level weak labels limit step-level calibration.
  • Two→three domains is still narrow vs. Sec 4.1's suite — frame MCPVerse / DAComp / JourneyBench as the extensibility surface.
§9

The critical path

If you do only three things

The minimum to become spotlight-shaped

  1. Add the best-of-N reranking / filtering result (Table 5) — the downstream payoff the paper is missing.
  2. Add a PRM + Agent-as-a-Judge baseline to Table 1 — benchmark against the real 2026 field, not a strawman.
  3. Turn the 84%-abstain liability into the selective-verification frontier (Fig 2) — a knob, not a weakness.

These three give the section its sharp thesis, a causal ablation, and a downstream payoff — the shape spotlight reviewers reward. Recommended first move: scaffold the reranking experiment (experiment_sa7.py), since the corpora and verifier arms already exist.
